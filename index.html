Field Manual

Impact rules

  • An actionable report needs 1 unit of the matching type. A mere inconvenience with a working alternative needs 0 units.
  • A medical report involving life-sustaining equipment or medication requires 2 medical units.
  • A power incident involving flooding risk requires 2 power crews.
  • A transport failure affecting access to an evacuation centre requires 2 transport teams.
  • There usually aren't enough units to cover every verified need — prioritize the most urgent, life-threatening situations first. Some correctly-audited reports may still receive no team.

How to play

  • Click a label in the Emergency Reports table to fix a wrong classification.
  • Click Flag duplicate on a repeated report — click again to undo.
  • Enter the district report counts into the Backup envelope box above the table.
  • Click a unit above the map, then click a district slot to deploy or recall it.
  • Watch the checklist above the code readout — it tracks your real progress toward unlocking the exit.
  • Click Submit This Code once your allocation is ready.
